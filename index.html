A small foundation with a clear job

Vital Strength Foundation is a nonprofit corporation incorporated in the Commonwealth of Virginia. We believe good care should not depend on what a person can afford, where they were born, or how old they are.

Our work runs on three commitments: make mental health care affordable, train the nurses who will deliver care, and protect the people at both ends of life, the young and the aged.

Incorporated March 18, 2026. In good standing with the Virginia State Corporation Commission.

What we fund

Four programs, each with a specific group of people in mind.

Mental health care subsidiesVirginia, USA

Therapy and psychiatric care can cost more than a household can carry. We subsidize part of that cost so people in Virginia can start treatment and stay in it.

  • Help with the cost of counseling and psychiatric visits
  • Open to Virginia residents who cannot afford full fees
  • Paid directly to the care provider

Nursing scholarshipsNigeria

Nigeria needs more well-trained nurses. We give scholarships to nursing students who have shown strong academic results and need financial help to finish their training.

  • Awarded on academic merit
  • For students enrolled in nursing programs in Nigeria
  • Applications reviewed by the Foundation's scholarship committee

Support for underprivileged childrenChildren

A child's start in life should not be limited by their family's income. We support children from low-income homes with the essentials that keep them healthy, in school, and cared for.

Support for the agedOlder adults

Older people often carry the heaviest costs with the least income. We help the aged live with dignity, with attention to their health, comfort, and companionship.

If you need care

Virginia residents seeking affordable mental health care can contact us to ask about subsidy support.

If you are a nursing student

Nigerian nursing students with strong grades can ask us for scholarship requirements and deadlines.
