Programs built around real needs.

Subsidized mental health care, scholarships for nursing students, and support for children and older people.

Four ways we help

Each program serves a specific group of people. Contact us for the current application requirements and dates.

Mental health care subsidiesVirginia, USA

We pay part of the cost of therapy, counseling and psychiatric care, so that cost does not keep anyone from starting treatment or finishing it. Payment goes directly to the care provider.

Who it is for

Virginia residents who need mental health care and cannot afford the full fee.

How to apply

Send us a short enquiry through the contact page. We will explain what we need and what the subsidy covers. Your details are kept private.

Nursing scholarshipsNigeria

We fund Nigerian nursing students with strong academic records who need financial help to complete their training, so that capable students are not held back by fees.

Who it is for

Students enrolled in a nursing program in Nigeria who have good grades and a demonstrated financial need.

How to apply

Email us with your name, school, level of study and most recent results, and we will send the application requirements and deadlines.

Support for underprivileged childrenChildren

We support children from low-income families with essentials that keep them healthy, in school and cared for.

Who it is for

Children whose families cannot meet basic needs. Families, schools, clinics and community groups can refer a child.

How to apply

Contact us with a brief description of the child's situation. We will tell you how referrals are assessed.

Support for the agedOlder adults

We help older people live with dignity, with attention to their health, comfort and companionship, especially those with little income or family support.

Who it is for

Older adults in need, and the families or carers looking after them.

How to apply

Contact us to refer yourself or someone you know. We will discuss what support is possible.
