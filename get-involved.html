Be part of the care.

Give, volunteer or partner with us, and help people who have too little.

Three ways to take part

Every form of help goes toward care, tuition and support for people who would otherwise go without.

DonateGive money

Give once or on a regular schedule. You can direct your gift to a single program, or to the area of greatest need.

  • Mental health care subsidies in Virginia
  • Nursing scholarships in Nigeria
  • Children's support
  • Support for the aged
Make a donation

Email us to receive payment details and to ask about donation receipts.

VolunteerGive time and skills

We welcome people who can contribute professional skills or a few hours.

  • Clinicians, counselors and nurses who can advise or mentor
  • Writers, designers and developers
  • Fundraising and event helpers
  • Visitors and companions for older people
Offer your time

PartnerWork with us

Clinics, nursing schools, churches, community groups and companies can partner with us to reach more people, refer those who need help, or sponsor a program.

Propose a partnership

How giving works

  1. Tell us how you want to help. Send a short email, or use the contact form.
  2. We confirm the details. We reply with payment instructions or next steps.
  3. Your help reaches a program. Funds go to the program you chose.

Not sure where to start?

Write to us and tell us what you have in mind. We will suggest a place where it can do the most good.
