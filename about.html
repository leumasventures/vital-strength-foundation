Care that does not depend on what you can afford.

Vital Strength Foundation connects people in Virginia and Nigeria with the health care, training and support they need at the points in life when they need it most.

Vital Strength Foundation was founded on a simple belief: strong communities are built by caring for the mind, the caregiver, the child and the elder.

In Virginia, we help people pay for mental health care so that cost does not stop them from seeking treatment. In Nigeria, we pay the way for high-achieving nursing students, who go on to care for others. Across both places, we support children from low-income families and older people who need help to live with dignity.

We are a young organization, and we plan carefully. Each program has a defined group of people, a clear purpose and a straightforward way to apply or give.

Our mission

To make mental health care affordable, to educate the next generation of nurses, and to protect the wellbeing of vulnerable children and the aged.

Our vision

A world where health, learning and care reach people because they need them, not because they can pay for them.

What guides our decisions

Dignity first

Everyone we help is treated with respect. We ask for what we need to assess a case and nothing more, and we keep personal information private.

Merit and need

Scholarships reward strong academic results. Subsidies and support go to those who cannot meet the cost themselves.

Honest stewardship

Donations are spent on the programs they were given for. We account for how funds are used and keep our records in good order.

Lasting impact

We back help that continues to work: a course of treatment completed, a degree finished, a child kept in school, an elder kept well.
